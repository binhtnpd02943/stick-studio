# UI v2: one design system, finished films first

The owner reviewed plan 5's UI in a real browser and rejected it. Each page had been built as a separate task and checked only for function and a11y. Nobody owned the visual system, so fonts mix, there is no hierarchy, internal terms leak onto the screen, and the review step means nothing to a creator. They approved the v2 mockups in `docs/superpowers/mockups/v2/`:

- `overview.dc.html`
- `film-detail.dc.html`
- `film-phone.dc.html`

These files hold the reference layout, copy and spacing. Each file's `<style>` block is the visual source of truth, and its `renderVals()` holds sample data.

## Design rules (binding on every page, not only the three above)
- One sans face, Be Vietnam Pro, for every word, headings included. JetBrains Mono only for numbers: durations, money, counts. The current mono `h1`/section labels and uppercase grey mono eyebrows are removed everywhere.
- Type scale (amended after review to what the approved mockups use): page title 28/600, section 18/600, body 15/400, secondary text and controls 14, meta 13/400, badges 12. Two single-use sizes: 16 for text inputs where the mockup sets it (the overview topic field; the login fields, which also keeps iOS from zooming) and 20 for the film title on the phone view. No other sizes except the brand mark and the studio floor's own art.
- Spacing on an 8px grid. Pages are `max-width: 1120px` with a 32px gutter (16px on phones). Sections are 48px apart.
- Colours: ink `#1b1b1b`, body `#4a4a4a`, muted `#767676`, line `#ececec`, soft surface `#f6f6f5`, accent `#ff8c26`, ok `#2f7a4f` on `#eaf5ee`, warn `#a15c07` on `#fff4e5`. Put them in `tokens.css`. AA contrast must still pass axe.
- Exactly one accent (orange) button per screen, for the main action. Secondary actions use an ink outline. Tertiary actions are quiet buttons (line border) or links.
- Buttons: 48px for main and secondary, 36–40px for quiet. Radius 10px for buttons, 12–16px for media and cards. No pill-shaped nav: the active item has a soft fill.
- Copy is written from the user's side. No internal terms on screen: no "Contact sheet", "Cổng QA", "initial", "v1", "explainer", raw step durations, or "Chờ duyệt". `vi.ts` stays the only source of text.
- Cards only where something is a separate object: film thumbnails, the running job, the redo box, script scenes. Not around whole sections.

## Changes
1. **Shell (`Layout`).**
   - Brand "Stick Studio" in sans.
   - Nav: Tổng quan, Phim, Nhân vật, Cài đặt. Duyệt is removed.
   - On the right: today's spend chip, "Hôm nay $x / $cap" with a 72px meter, plus an avatar button. The avatar opens a small menu with Đăng xuất.
   - Keep the phone bottom nav, updated to match.
2. **Overview** (follow `overview.dc.html`).
   - "Hôm nay làm phim gì?" with an inline topic field and a "Làm phim" button (the one accent). It creates the film with the topic as its title (existing create mutation plus Idempotency-Key) and navigates to it. A hint line shows the time and approximate cost.
   - "Đang làm · n": one row per queued or running film. Each row shows a small figure, the title, who is working ("Tùng đang viết kịch bản"; reuse the studio `TEAM` and a per-step verb in `vi.ts`), an 8-segment progress (done ink, current accent) with aria-label "Bước k trên 8", and a quiet "Xem đội làm việc" link to the film. If there are none, show one muted line of text, not an empty-state illustration.
   - "Vừa xong · n": a 4-column grid of finished films (awaiting_review, approved, published, qa_failed), newest first, at most 8, plus a link "Tất cả phim".
     - Each card: a 9:16 thumbnail drawn by the existing engine canvas, with a duration badge; title; "$cost · relative time"; and the actions "Tải về" (download of the final MP4), "Đăng", and "⋯" (menu: Làm lại → film page redo box).
     - A qa_failed card gets a "Cần xem lại" flag, and its first action becomes "Xem lỗi", linking to the film's Chất lượng tab.
     - **Bug:** the thumbnails currently render as tall blank boxes. Find out why the timeline canvas draws nothing there, and fix it. Each card is capped at about 340px tall.
   - The cost block and the review-preview section are removed. Spend lives in the header.
3. **Film detail** (follow `film-detail.dc.html`).
   - Breadcrumb "Phim / title".
   - Finished film: a 360px 9:16 player on the left. On the right:
     - Title 28/600.
     - One meta line: duration · n cảnh · cast names · $cost · relative finish time.
     - A chip: "✓ Đạt kiểm tra chất lượng", or a warn chip with the QA issue count.
     - Actions: "Tải video MP4" (accent), and "Đăng lên TikTok" (outline). Publishing is not built, so the button is disabled, with a note and a link to Cài đặt.
     - The "Chưa ưng? Làm lại với góp ý" box: a textarea (the redo reason), a "Sửa từ" select with user words (Cốt truyện, Kịch bản, Lồng tiếng, Hình ảnh mapped to story, screenplay, voice, render), and a quiet "Làm lại" button. It uses the existing redo API.
   - Tabs: Kịch bản (scene cards in 3 columns: "Cảnh n", length, place, lines with a cast-coloured dot and name), Chất lượng (the former QA tab, in user words), Các bản (n) (versions).
   - Keep the screenplay edit ("Sửa") inside the Kịch bản tab as a quiet button.
   - While queued or running: the studio floor at full width, with under it one line "Tùng đang viết kịch bản · Bước k trên 8" and the segmented progress. `StepTimeline` stays in the DOM, collapsed under a "Chi tiết các bước" disclosure, so the e2e `[data-step]` rows still exist. No video and no actions until the film is done, apart from "Huỷ" (quiet).
   - Failed, blocked or cancelled: a clear message in user words, plus "Thử lại" (accent) using redo from the failed step.
4. **Phone** (`film-phone.dc.html`).
   - At widths under 720px, film detail stacks: the video fills the width, then title and meta, then a sticky bottom dock with Làm lại · Đăng · Tải về. Tải về is the accent. Làm lại opens the redo box as a sheet.
   - Swiping left or right on the video moves to the next or previous finished film. Reuse `swipe.ts`.
5. **Review is retired as a page.** Delete `Review.tsx`, `ReviewCard`, their tests and the nav item. `/review` redirects to `/`.
   - The backend review queue stays untouched; the web no longer uses it.
   - Status words: awaiting_review and approved both read "Xong". published reads "Đã đăng". qa_failed reads "Cần xem lại".
   - Record this as a deviation in `docs/superpowers/plans/2026-11-22-plan-5-followups.md`. The spec's human review gate before auto-publish returns, as an opt-in step, when TikTok publishing is built.
6. **Films list (`/films`), Cast, Settings, Login.** Apply the design rules: type, spacing, buttons, no mono headings. No layout redesign beyond that.
7. **Fake mode title mismatch.**
   - With `STICK_FAKE=1`, the fake LLM returns a canned story ("Phân số là gì?") whatever the topic, so the film's title and its video disagree.
   - Make the fake story's title (and its opening line, where trivial) come from the film's title or brief. The fake stays deterministic.
   - Find it under `packages/providers`.
